## Section 8 — FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions About Selling Your House for Cash

### How fast can you buy my house?
In many cases we can close in as little as 7 to 14 days once you accept our offer, depending on the title work. If you need more time to move, you can choose a later closing date that works for you.

### Do I need to make any repairs or clean the house?
No. We buy houses as-is, in any condition. You don't need to make repairs, clean, or paint. You can also leave behind any furniture or belongings you don't want to take with you.

### How do you decide how much to offer?
We look at the location, the condition of the house, the cost of any repairs it needs, and recent sales of similar homes nearby. We'll walk you through how we arrived at the number so you can make an informed decision.

### Are there any fees or commissions?
No. When you sell directly to Turning Point Homes there are no real estate agent commissions and no fees charged by us. We'll go over all closing costs with you before you sign anything.

### Is there any obligation if I request an offer?
No. Requesting a cash offer is free and there is no obligation to accept it. If the offer doesn't work for you, you can simply say no.

### Will I get less than if I list with an agent?
Our cash offer is usually below what a fully updated house might sell for on the open market. But when you sell to us you skip agent commissions, repairs, holding costs, and months of waiting, so many sellers find the difference is smaller than they expected. We'll be honest if listing makes more sense for you.

### Can you buy a house that is in foreclosure or behind on payments?
Often, yes. If you are behind on mortgage payments or facing foreclosure, a quick cash sale may help you pay off the loan before the foreclosure sale date. The sooner you contact us, the more options you have.

### Can I sell a house I inherited or that is in probate?
Yes. We regularly buy inherited houses, including houses that are full of belongings or need work. If the estate is in probate, we can work with the personal representative and the estate's attorney on timing.

### Do you buy rental properties with tenants living in them?
Yes. We buy rental houses with or without tenants in place, so you don't have to wait for a lease to end or deal with an eviction before selling.

### What areas do you buy houses in?
We buy houses in Independence, Kansas City, Blue Springs, Lee's Summit, Raytown, Grain Valley, and surrounding communities in the Kansas City metro area.
