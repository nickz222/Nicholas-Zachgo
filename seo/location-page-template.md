# Location Pages: Independence Example + Template

Location pages help you show up for "we buy houses [city]" and "sell my house fast [city]" searches. Google ignores (or penalizes) pages that are the same text with the city name swapped. Each page needs real, local, useful content — aim for 600–1,000 words, with at least half of it unique to that city.

**Rules for every location page**
1. Only create a page for a city you really buy in.
2. At least 3 sections must be specific to that city (neighborhoods, types of housing, local situations, real deals you've done there).
3. Never invent deals, testimonials, or statistics. Use [FILL IN] until you have real ones, and leave the section out if you don't.
4. Use the same NAP (name, address, phone) in the footer as everywhere else.
5. Put the lead form (or a big button to it) near the top.
6. Link to /sell-your-house/ and to 1–2 neighboring city pages.
7. Add the page to the footer "Areas We Serve" list so it isn't an orphan.

Don't copy the neighborhood/local details below into other cities' pages — research each city.

## Houses We Commonly Buy in Independence

Independence has a lot of housing that was built from the early 1900s through the 1970s. Many of these homes are solid but have issues that make a traditional sale harder:

- **Older homes with foundation or basement water problems.** Cracked block walls, bowing basement walls, and wet basements are common in older Jackson County houses. Buyers using FHA or VA loans often can't close on a house with these problems. We can.
- **Outdated houses that need a full update.** Original kitchens, old wiring, galvanized plumbing, or an old furnace. You don't need to update anything before selling to us.
- **Inherited houses.** Many Independence homes have been owned by the same family for decades. If you've inherited your parents' house and it's full of belongings, you can leave what you don't want. See our page on [selling an inherited house](/sell-inherited-house-kansas-city/).
- **Rental houses.** Independence has many single-family rentals and duplexes. We buy them with tenants in place. See [selling a rental property](/sell-rental-property-kansas-city/).

## Cash Sale vs. Listing Your Independence House

Listing with an agent can get you a higher price if your house is updated and you can wait. But on an older Independence house that needs work, the costs add up: repairs before listing, repair requests after inspection, agent commissions, months of utilities, taxes, and insurance while it sits, and the risk that the buyer's loan falls through. A cash sale trades some of the top-dollar price for speed, certainty, and zero repairs. We'll give you an honest view of which option makes more sense for your house.
