## 1. Address and service area (do this first)

**Fix the address** to exactly: `25209 E Blue Mills Rd, Independence, MO 64058`. (Edit profile → Business information → Location.) Use the same spelling and abbreviations ("E", "Rd") as your website footer and other listings.

**Should you show or hide the address?** Google's guidelines say:
- If customers come to your address during business hours (a staffed office), you can show it.
- If you serve customers at *their* location and don't have customers come to you (for example, you work from home and visit sellers' houses), you're a **service-area business** and should **hide the address** and set a service area instead. Google does not allow home addresses to be shown for businesses that don't receive customers there.

For a "we buy houses" business run from a home, the right setup is almost certainly:
- Business location: enter 25209 E Blue Mills Rd (Google still needs it for verification), then choose **don't show the address to customers** (wording varies — look for "Show business address to customers" and turn it OFF, or remove the address while keeping a service area).
- **Service area:** add up to 20 areas. Recommended: Independence MO, Kansas City MO, Blue Springs MO, Lee's Summit MO, Raytown MO, Grain Valley MO, Sugar Creek MO, Buckner MO, Oak Grove MO, Grandview MO, Jackson County MO. Google recommends the service area not extend beyond about 2 hours' drive from your base. Only list places you really buy in.
- Hiding the address in GBP does not affect the address on your website. You can keep it on the site and in the schema, or switch the site to "Serving Independence & the Kansas City metro" if you'd rather keep your home address private. Pick one approach and keep it consistent.

**Verification warning:** changing the address or business name can trigger re-verification (often a video verification now). Have ready: proof of business (e.g., business registration with the Missouri Secretary of State, signage or vehicle branding, business cards, a business bank statement or utility bill showing the business name). **Real estate investor / "we buy houses" profiles are a category Google scrutinizes for spam, so make sure everything on the profile is accurate and verifiable.**

## 10. Getting reviews (compliant)

Reviews are one of the biggest local ranking and trust factors. Rules:
- **Ask every seller** (and maybe partners like title officers or contractors who actually did business with you) — not only the ones you think are happy. Selectively asking only happy customers ("review gating") goes against Google's policy.
- **No incentives** of any kind (no gift cards, discounts, or "$50 off closing costs for a review"). This violates Google policy and, in the US, the FTC's rule on fake reviews and testimonials.
- **No fake reviews**, no reviews from family/friends who weren't customers, no writing reviews for people, no review-swapping with other businesses.
- Don't ask for reviews at a seller's kitchen table on your phone or from your Wi-Fi in a batch — Google may filter them. Let them post from their own device.
- **Reply to every review**, good or bad, within a few days. Keep replies short, polite, and never share private details of the deal.
- Only text people who gave you their number and agreed to be contacted; include an opt-out line in texts.

**Get your review link:** in your Business Profile, click **Ask for reviews** (or "Get more reviews") and copy the short link. Paste it below where it says `[REVIEW LINK]`.

## 12. Guideline warnings (things that get profiles suspended)

- **No keywords in the business name** (e.g., "We Buy Houses KC – Turning Point Homes"). Use your real name only.
- **No virtual offices, PO boxes, or UPS Store mailboxes** as your address. Use your real location and hide it if customers don't come there.
- **Don't show a home address** if you don't receive customers there — use the service-area setup.
- **One profile per business.** Don't create extra profiles for Blue Springs, Lee's Summit, etc. unless you have a real, staffed office in each. Use location *pages on your website* instead.
- **No fake, incentivized, or gated reviews.** No buying reviews. Don't ask employees or family to review.
- **No URLs, phone numbers, or promotional language** ("BEST cash offers in KC!!!") in the description.
- **No phone numbers in post text** (posts may be rejected); use the Call button.
- **Accurate categories only.** Don't choose "Real estate agent" if you're not licensed.
- **Be careful editing many fields at once** — big changes (name + address + category together) can trigger a suspension or re-verification. Make the address fix first, wait until it's approved, then do categories, then the rest.
- **Hours must be real** hours you're available.
- Watch for **"suggested edits"** from Google or the public (shown in your dashboard) and reject any that are wrong.
- If the profile gets suspended, don't create a new one — use Google's reinstatement form with your proof of business.
